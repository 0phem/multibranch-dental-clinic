## 5. How should I take care of my teeth at home?

**Current answer:** General tips: brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste for about two minutes, clean between your teeth every day (for example with floss), limit sugary food and drinks, and keep regular check-ups. For advice about your own teeth, book a consultation.
