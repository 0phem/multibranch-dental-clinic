# Canonical 25-Module Coverage

Domain baseline `dac864e` (through Phase 3.5); the Patient presentation and M24 were added in Phase 4B. **Implemented** means frontend behavior with local persistence, not backend/integration completeness. **Partial** identifies a materially narrower frontend representation than the canonical module/ERD scope. **Planned** means the module is defined but not yet built. M1–M23 are Core: M7, M13, M14, M15 and M22 are newly defined by the canonical restructure, and most of them are not built yet — their Status says so. M24/M25 are Proposed Enhancements (PE) (formerly recorded as Approved Frontend Enhancements): M24 is an implemented prototype (Phase 4B.2) and M25 has only a limited frontend preview today (full implementation is deferred). Shared screens do not merge modules.

| M5 — Treatment & Clinical Workflow Management | Core / Server-authoritative (Laravel/PostgreSQL) | One server Treatment per Visit, started by the responsible Dentist from a Called / Treatment Ready queue entry in one server transaction (queue Served, Visit and appointment In Treatment, Treatment created); whole-documentation saves with procedure lines (canonical service, quantity, notes; no prices) and immutable per-revision history; completion Completes the Treatment, Visit and appointment together. | M6 — Appointment Booking & Smart Scheduling Management | Core / Server-authoritative (Laravel/PostgreSQL) | Patient Book (Smart Find / Manual booking) and Staff/Owner appointment management read and write appointments only through the Laravel M6 API: availability, Smart Scheduling recommendation, create, reschedule, cancel and the pre-arrival No-show (Check-In is M8; treatment start/completion are M5 Treatment commands that move the linked appointment in the same server transaction). **Smart Scheduling and conflict prevention** (formerly M7): the server validates branch/service/Dentist eligibility, shifts, hours, the Patient window and start grid, and assigns the Dentist deterministically (fewest booked minutes, then Dentist id); PostgreSQL exclusion constraints prevent concurrent Dentist/Patient overlaps. No payment preference is collected at booking (payment belongs to M11; the card-paid cancellation rule is deferred with it). Branch closures and location ranking wait for authoritative M2 data (no coordinates are fabricated, so Smart Find asks the Patient to choose a branch). Approved transitional follow-up rule (until M20 is backend-authoritative): the Dentist records/recommends the required follow-up in the treatment encounter; clinic Staff (or the Owner, where current authorization permits) schedule it as a normal M6 server appointment whose public id is stored on the local follow-up. The appointment list is a bounded working window (about three months back and ahead), never all-time history; the Analytics Scheduling metric names that window. Pre-cutover browser appointments are not uploaded; records that referenced them are read-only legacy history excluded from live KPIs |
| M7 — Patient AI Chatbot Management | Core (new) / Limited preview | A Patient-only Clinic Assistant with three fixed, rule-based answers (booking, HMO documents, queue) | Not yet an AI chatbot: no language model, backend endpoint or Patient-data access exists. Only today's appointments (Asia/Manila); one active Visit per Patient; walk-ins reserve no Dentist appointment time. The Visit / Clinical Encounter is shared infrastructure, not a module. No Patient self-Check-In and no Patient Visit API yet. Leaving/cancelling after Check-In is not available until a Visit/Queue clinic policy is decided; No-show is a pre-arrival appointment decision |
| M9 — Patient Queue Management | Core / Server-authoritative (Laravel/PostgreSQL) | Server queue anchored to the Visit: entries created with the M8 arrival (one per Visit), server-issued numbers per branch + Dentist + clinic day, server-computed positions; Staff/Owner Call/Ready/Away/Return and operational priority with reason and history; the Dentist may Call their own entries; starting treatment (the M5 Treatment start) Serves the entry with the Visit in one transaction; each Patient reads only their own current queue state; screens refresh every 30 s while visible | Served ends the queue experience; the Visit owns In Treatment/Completed and the appointment owns the pre-arrival No-show. A Visit without a responsible Dentist is not queued (no assignment workflow yet). Leaving after Check-In and end-of-day handling await a clinic decision. Wait estimates and capacity stay client-side M10 calculations. Operational Owner exceptions are documented separately |
| M10 — Clinic Capacity, Waiting-Time & Workforce Management | Core / Implemented frontend estimates | Queue wait estimate, load/threshold and alternative-branch visibility; Staff/Owner capacity, workload and cross-branch alternatives (formerly M15) | Aggregate estimates differ from individual queue order; no guaranteed case-specific duration. Read operational estimates only; no automatic Staff or Patient reassignment across branches |

No arbitrary bill, partial payment or refund; zero-fee closure unresolved; approved HMO coverage is not yet applied to bills. ## Module restructure (old → new)

The canonical structure replaced the earlier numbering. Historical phase documents keep their original numbers.

| Earlier module | Now |
| --- | --- |
| M1 User, Role & Access Management | M1 User & Access Management |
| M6 Core Appointment Booking & Schedule Management | M6 Appointment Booking & Smart Scheduling Management |
| M7 Smart Scheduling & Conflict Prevention | Feature of M6 (the number M7 is reused for Patient AI Chatbot Management) |
| M9 Smart Patient Queue Management | M9 Patient Queue Management |
| M10 Waiting-Time & Patient Flow Capacity Management | M10 Clinic Capacity, Waiting-Time & Workforce Management |
| M11 Billing & Payment Management | M11 Billing, Payment & Receipt Management |
| M12 HMO Verification & Documentation Management | M12 HMO Case, Coverage & Follow-Up Management |

| M14 HMO Follow-Up & Escalation Automation | Part of M12 (M14 is reused for Patient Forms, Documents & Consent Management) |
| M15 Staff Workload & Cross-Branch Capacity Automation | Part of M10 (M15 is reused for Clinic Configuration & Business Rules Management) |
| M18 Real-Time Patient Notification Management | M18 Real-Time Notification & Reminder Management |
| M19 Digital Prescription Management | M19 Digital Prescription & OCR-Assisted Management |
| M20 Treatment Follow-Up Scheduling Management | M20 Treatment Follow-Up & Recall Management |

| M24 / M25 (formerly Approved Frontend Enhancements) | Proposed Enhancements (PE) |

**Recorded events.** Because five numbers were reused, module numbers are no longer stored as the identity of workflow/audit records or automation targets. New records store a stable domain key (for example `hmo`, `appointment`, `treatment→billing`), and the displayed module number is derived from the current structure (`src/module-map.js`). Records saved before the restructure are read through a fixed legacy table, so an old M13 or M14 HMO event still displays as M12, never as the new M13 or M14.

## Responsibilities and separations

- **M6:** booking, Smart Scheduling, conflict prevention and automatic Dentist assignment are one module; validation stays deterministic.
- **M8 / M9 / M10:** arrival admission / individual order and state / aggregate wait, capacity and workforce.
- **M12:** one HMO lifecycle whose sub-processes keep distinct meanings — local preparation, external submission and provider response, follow-up and escalation.
- **M13 / M14 / M15:** service catalog and pricing / documents, consent and extraction infrastructure / validated clinic configuration.
- **M17 / M18:** human conversation with participants / one-way operational events with recipients.
- **M21 / M22 / M23:** analyze what happened and present Owner oversight / record who did what / coordinate the next predefined administrative action.
- **Document extraction (future):** OCR is not a module. M14 stores and extracts; M12 interprets HMO responses with Staff confirmation; M19 drafts prescriptions for Dentist authorization. Output is always a draft.

## Role surfaces

Patient: Home, Book (Smart Find / Manual booking), Visits, Menu (Live Queue is contextual). Menu is grouped —
Bookings (Book Appointment, Visits), Communications (Messages), Account (Receipts & Payments, My Profile) — with
a confirmed Logout separated at the bottom; My Profile itself still lists Prescriptions, Follow-Ups, HMO and
Referral & Loyalty (M24) under its own "More" section, unchanged. The Patient-only Clinic Assistant (M7 preview) floats on Patient screens.

Staff: operational worklists as allowed by current subrole and assigned branch. Receptionist, Dental Assistant, Cashier, HMO Coordinator and Patient Engagement Staff do not have interchangeable permission sets. See the [role matrix](DOCUMENTATION_RECONCILIATION.md#role-and-permission-clarifications).

Dentist: schedule, own queue, scoped Patient chart, exact Treatment, Prescriptions, Follow-Ups and participant Messages. No Staff payment/HMO processing authority.

Owner/Admin: Dashboard, Analytics, Branches, Team, Capacity, User Management, HMO Management (read-only oversight) and Automation Monitor; Engagement (M24 administration, M25 preview). Shared Phase 1 administrative exceptions do not imply clinical authority. **25-Module Coverage is a defense document, not production navigation.**
